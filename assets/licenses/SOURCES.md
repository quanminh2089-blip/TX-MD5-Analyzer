# Third-party asset sources

This is an original small survival FPS prototype, not an extraction from Genshin or another commercial game.

## Kenney models and sounds (CC0)

- Weapons, grass, clouds and sound effects: https://github.com/KenneyNL/Starter-Kit-FPS (downloaded 2026-11-02).
- Pack listing: https://kenney.nl/assets/blaster-kit
- External environment models (pine/oak/rocks/house/tower): https://github.com/syuhei176/ai-game-assets at ebfd758dea8db5793c765cc72564efadb36a4ed0.
- Original environment packs: https://kenney.nl/assets/nature-kit and Kenney City Kit / Tower Defense Kit.
- Kenney confirms asset pack licensing: https://kenney.nl/support
- Source code from Starter Kit FPS is NOT used; its original MIT license is retained as provenance. Included assets are CC0 as stated by its README.
